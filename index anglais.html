Student Passport

Student Passport and transversal skills

The Student Passport is a course on Moodle: mandatory, but ungraded. Passing it is the key that unlocks your transversal skills.

1

Mandatory, but ungraded

You must pass it on Moodle, but it does not count towards your overall GPA.

2

Late? Do it anyway

The deadline is September 15th, but you can still complete it afterwards. The main thing is to pass it.

3

It unlocks the rest

Passing it opens up your first transversal skill. This is a mandatory and graded course.

Stuck on your Passport?

A mutual aid forum is here for your questions. Students who have already been through it are the ones answering you.

Transversal skills

An online, mandatory, and graded course common to all undergraduate students, spread across the three years.

Individually, at your own pace

Alone or in groups. Tip: do it ahead of time and don't discover the course the night before exams.

Watch out for deadlines

Some modules have strict deadlines. Log in regularly so you don't miss anything.

It counts towards your average

Pass the modules and pay close attention to the exercises: they often show up on exams.

In L1, your first transversal skill, Written Competence, only unlocks after you have passed your Student Passport. So remember to pass it first.
